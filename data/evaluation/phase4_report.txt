======================================================================
  YOLO11n CAPABILITY GAP: DOORS AND STAIRS
======================================================================
  YOLO11n is trained on COCO 80 classes.
  Neither 'door', 'stairs', 'doorway', nor 'step'
  is a COCO class.

  This means YOLO11n cannot reliably detect doors or stairs.
  It may produce:
    - Zero detections  (most likely)
    - Misclassified detections (e.g. door as 'refrigerator' or 'tv')
    - Very low confidence incidental detections

  DO NOT interpret a non-zero detection as reliable door/stair detection.
  This is a documented architectural limitation of YOLO11n for this use case.
